UDPcast
=======

Purpose:
  Reliable multicast image distribution used by ZOS group deployment.

Upstream:
  https://www.udpcast.linux.lu/

Source used for the bundled binaries:
  https://github.com/a1ive/udpcast

Bundled files:
  windows-x64/udp-sender.exe
  linux-x86_64/udp-sender
  linux-aarch64/ (native package fallback/auto-install)
  linux-loongarch64/ (native package fallback/auto-install)
  ../../third_party/udpcast-msvc-source.zip
